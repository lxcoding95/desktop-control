# Support

Desktop Control is **not yet a working product**. No installer, public server, CLI, or usable MCP tools are available.

For repository planning questions or improvement proposals, open an [issue](https://github.com/lxcoding95/desktop-control/issues/new/choose). Check [README](README.md) and [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) first.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not publicly post a proof of concept containing exploitable details.

No support response-time guarantee, service uptime commitment or paid support tier exists.
